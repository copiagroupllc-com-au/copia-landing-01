About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management firm supporting innovative companies across AI, fintech, blockchain, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.
Through strategic investments and partnerships, we help innovative businesses deliver world-class digital products and exceptional customer experiences. Our portfolio companies build enterprise software, SaaS platforms, fintech solutions, blockchain applications, and AI-powered technologies serving customers worldwide.
We are seeking an experienced Senior Customer Support Specialist to deliver exceptional customer service, resolve complex technical and product-related issues, and help drive continuous improvements across our customer support operations.
________________


The Opportunity
As a Senior Customer Support Specialist, you will serve as a trusted point of contact for customers, ensuring timely issue resolution and outstanding service experiences. You'll collaborate closely with Product, Engineering, QA, Customer Success, and Operations teams to troubleshoot technical issues, improve support processes, and advocate for customer needs.
This role offers the opportunity to work with cutting-edge technology products while contributing to customer satisfaction, operational excellence, and business growth.
________________


What You'll Do
Customer Support
* Provide professional, timely, and empathetic support via email, chat, phone, and ticketing systems
* Resolve customer inquiries related to products, services, billing, accounts, and technical issues
* Investigate and troubleshoot complex software and platform-related problems
* Escalate critical issues to Engineering or Product teams when necessary
* Ensure customer issues are resolved within established Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
Technical Troubleshooting
* Diagnose application, account, API, and platform-related issues
* Assist customers with onboarding, account setup, and product configuration
* Reproduce reported issues and document findings for engineering teams
* Monitor support trends and identify recurring product issues
* Maintain detailed case documentation and knowledge base articles
Customer Experience
* Deliver exceptional customer service that exceeds expectations
* Build strong relationships with customers through clear communication and problem-solving
* Gather customer feedback and share product improvement recommendations
* Educate customers on product features and best practices
* Promote customer retention through proactive engagement and issue resolution
Process Improvement
* Contribute to the development of support documentation, FAQs, and internal knowledge bases
* Recommend workflow improvements to increase support efficiency
* Participate in quality assurance reviews and process optimization initiatives
* Help define customer support best practices and operational standards
* Support training and onboarding of junior support specialists
Cross-Functional Collaboration
* Work closely with Engineering to resolve technical incidents
* Partner with Product teams to communicate customer feedback and feature requests
* Collaborate with QA to verify bug fixes and product releases
* Coordinate with Customer Success and Sales teams to ensure seamless customer experiences
* Participate in incident response and post-incident reviews when required
