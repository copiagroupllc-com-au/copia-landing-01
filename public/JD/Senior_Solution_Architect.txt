The Opportunity
As a Senior Solution Architect, you will serve as a trusted technical advisor, working closely with business stakeholders, Product Managers, Engineering teams, and executive leadership to define solution architectures for complex technology initiatives. You'll lead architecture design, guide implementation strategies, and ensure solutions are scalable, secure, and aligned with long-term business goals.
This role combines deep technical expertise with strategic thinking, offering the opportunity to influence enterprise architecture while mentoring engineering teams across multiple products and platforms.
