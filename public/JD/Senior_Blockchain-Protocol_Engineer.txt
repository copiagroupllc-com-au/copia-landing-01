About Copia Group
Copia Group is building a Stripe-like infrastructure for Web3 — a next-generation fintech and payments platform designed to make blockchain-based payments, digital assets, and financial services easier to build and use.
As we expand internationally, including our planned presence in Australia, we're building a strong engineering team focused on secure, scalable blockchain infrastructure and real-world financial applications.
The Role
We are looking for a Senior Blockchain / Protocol Engineer to take ownership of the architecture and development of blockchain protocols and smart-contract infrastructure powering our Web3 fintech platform.
You will work across EVM-compatible networks and emerging blockchain ecosystems, designing secure protocols, smart contracts, transaction infrastructure, and integrations.
This is a hands-on engineering role with significant ownership. You will work closely with backend, frontend, product, and security engineers to take blockchain functionality from architecture through production.
Key Responsibilities
* Design and develop smart contracts and blockchain protocols for Web3 fintech and payment applications.
* Architect secure and scalable DeFi, payment, tokenization, and RWA infrastructure.
* Develop and maintain Solidity-based smart contracts for EVM networks.
* Research and evaluate blockchain technologies, L2s, interoperability solutions, and emerging protocols.
* Design blockchain integrations, bridges, transaction flows, and on-chain/off-chain architectures.
* Build backend services that interact with smart contracts and blockchain networks.
* Review smart-contract architecture and identify potential security and economic risks.
* Develop comprehensive unit, integration, fuzzing, and invariant tests.
* Participate in protocol audits and security reviews.
* Optimize gas usage, transaction throughput, reliability, and protocol performance.
* Work with cryptographic primitives and formal verification where appropriate.
* Collaborate with product and engineering leadership to translate business requirements into technical architecture.
* Mentor engineers and contribute to engineering standards and best practices.
* Take ownership of blockchain features from architecture → development → testing → deployment → production.
