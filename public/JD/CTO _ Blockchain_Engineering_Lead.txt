About Copia Group
Copia Group is building a next-generation Web3 fintech and payments platform — essentially “Stripe for Web3.”
Our goal is to make blockchain-based financial infrastructure easier for businesses, developers, and financial institutions to integrate, while providing reliable infrastructure for payments, digital assets, liquidity, and financial products.
As we expand globally, including establishing our presence in Australia, we're looking for a technically exceptional leader to help shape the blockchain architecture and engineering organization from the ground up.

The Role
We are looking for a CTO / Blockchain Engineering Lead to own the technical direction of our blockchain and Web3 infrastructure.
This is a hands-on leadership role. You will not simply manage engineering teams — you'll be expected to participate in architecture decisions, protocol design, technical strategy, security, infrastructure, and critical implementation work.
You will work closely with product, business, and engineering stakeholders to turn the company's Web3 fintech vision into a scalable production platform.
The ideal candidate has deep experience with blockchain protocols, EVM, distributed systems, wallets, payments, digital assets, and/or financial infrastructure, combined with experience leading engineering teams or building products from zero to production.
