The Opportunity
As a Senior Web3 Backend Engineer, you will build mission-critical backend services that interact with blockchain networks, smart contracts, wallets, and decentralized protocols. You'll collaborate closely with Smart Contract Engineers, Frontend Developers, DevOps Engineers, Product Managers, and Security teams to deliver scalable, reliable, and secure Web3 applications.
