About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management and technology-focused firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.
We collaborate on projects connected to Stripe’s ecosystem, helping build scalable payment systems, developer-focused platforms, financial infrastructure, and next-generation commerce solutions used by businesses globally.
We are expanding our product organization and seeking experienced product leaders passionate about building modern financial technology products and scalable digital platforms.

The Opportunity
We are seeking a Senior Product Manager to lead product strategy, roadmap execution, and cross-functional delivery across fintech and commerce platform initiatives.
In this role, you will work closely with engineering, design, operations, and leadership teams to define product vision, prioritize features, and deliver user-centric solutions that support modern payment and infrastructure platforms.
This role offers strong ownership, strategic influence, and the opportunity to shape high-impact products used at scale.
