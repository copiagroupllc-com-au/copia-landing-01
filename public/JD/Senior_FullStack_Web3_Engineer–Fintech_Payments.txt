About Copia Group
Copia Group is building a next-generation Web3 fintech and payments platform — essentially “Stripe for Web3.”
Our goal is to make blockchain-based payments and financial infrastructure easier, faster, and more accessible for businesses and users. As we expand internationally, including our Australia operations, we are building a strong engineering team to develop secure, scalable, production-ready Web3 infrastructure.
We are looking for a Senior Full Stack Web3 Engineer who can work across smart contracts, backend services, APIs, and modern frontend applications.
About the Role
As a Senior Full Stack Web3 Engineer, you will be responsible for designing and building core components of our Web3 fintech and payments platform.
You will work across the entire technology stack — from Solidity smart contracts and blockchain integrations to backend APIs and frontend applications.
This is a hands-on engineering role for someone who understands both traditional software engineering and decentralized technologies.
You will collaborate closely with product, engineering, and infrastructure teams to build reliable payment and financial workflows on blockchain networks.
Key Responsibilities
* Design, develop, test, and deploy production-grade Web3 applications and payment infrastructure.
* Develop and maintain Solidity smart contracts and supporting blockchain services.
* Build secure and scalable backend services using technologies such as Node.js, TypeScript, Go, or Java.
* Develop modern frontend applications using React, Next.js, or similar frameworks.
* Integrate blockchain networks, wallets, smart contracts, exchanges, payment providers, and external APIs.
* Build APIs and services connecting Web3 infrastructure with traditional fintech systems.
* Design transaction, payment, settlement, wallet, and token-related workflows.
* Work with EVM-compatible networks, including Ethereum and Layer 2 ecosystems.
* Implement smart-contract testing, security testing, monitoring, and deployment automation.
* Work with blockchain indexing and data infrastructure where required.
* Collaborate with DevOps engineers to deploy and operate production systems.
* Participate in architecture decisions and technical design reviews.
* Identify performance, scalability, and security issues and implement appropriate solutions.
* Write clean, maintainable, well-tested production code.
* Review code and provide technical guidance to other engineers.
* Contribute to engineering standards, documentation, CI/CD, and development processes.
