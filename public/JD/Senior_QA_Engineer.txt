The Copia Group is an investment management and technology-focused firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.

The Opportunity
We are seeking a Senior QA Engineer to help ensure the quality, reliability, and performance of modern fintech and commerce platforms.
You will work closely with engineering, product, and design teams to develop testing strategies, improve quality processes, and deliver stable, scalable applications across web platforms, APIs, and backend systems.
This role offers strong ownership, cross-functional collaboration, and the opportunity to contribute directly to high-impact infrastructure products.
