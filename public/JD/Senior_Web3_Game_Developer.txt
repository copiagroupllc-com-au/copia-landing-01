The Opportunity
As a Senior Web3 Game Developer, you will design and develop high-performance multiplayer games while integrating blockchain technologies such as NFTs, digital assets, wallets, and smart contracts. You'll collaborate closely with Game Designers, Artists, Blockchain Engineers, Backend Developers, Product Managers, and DevOps teams to deliver engaging gaming experiences powered by Web3.
This role offers significant ownership over gameplay systems, blockchain integration, technical architecture, and live game infrastructure while providing opportunities to mentor engineers and influence the future of decentralized gaming.
