The Opportunity
As a Senior Data Engineer, you will play a critical role in architecting and scaling data infrastructure that powers reporting, analytics, machine learning initiatives, and business operations.
You will collaborate closely with software engineers, data analysts, product teams, and stakeholders to build reliable data pipelines, optimize data systems, and ensure high standards for data quality and accessibility.
This role offers significant technical ownership and the opportunity to shape the future of data engineering within a growing technology ecosystem.
