The Copia Group is an investment management firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, digital infrastructure, commerce, AI, and emerging technology sectors.
Through strategic investments and partnerships, we help accelerate the development of modern financial services, digital platforms, and customer-centric technology solutions. Our portfolio companies build products that prioritize usability, accessibility, and exceptional user experiences across web and mobile platforms.
We are seeking an experienced Senior UI/UX Designer to create intuitive, visually engaging, and user-focused digital experiences that transform complex ideas into elegant product solutions.

The Opportunity
As a Senior UI/UX Designer, you will play a key role in shaping the user experience of modern web and mobile applications. You'll collaborate closely with Product Managers, Software Engineers, Researchers, and stakeholders to design intuitive interfaces that balance business objectives with user needs.
This role offers the opportunity to influence product strategy, establish design standards, and contribute throughout the entire product development lifecycle—from discovery and research to prototyping, testing, and implementation.
________________


What You'll Do
User Experience Design
* Design intuitive, user-centered experiences across web and mobile platforms
* Conduct user research, competitive analysis, and usability testing
* Develop user flows, journey maps, wireframes, and interactive prototypes
* Translate business requirements into seamless user experiences
* Continuously improve products through user feedback and data-driven design decisions
User Interface Design
* Create high-fidelity UI designs that align with product goals and brand identity
* Design responsive interfaces for desktop, tablet, and mobile applications
* Build reusable design components and maintain consistency across products
* Collaborate with engineers to ensure pixel-perfect implementation
* Ensure designs meet accessibility (WCAG) and usability standards
Collaboration & Product Development
* Partner with Product Managers to define product vision and user requirements
* Work closely with engineering teams throughout development and release cycles
* Present design concepts and communicate design rationale to stakeholders
* Participate in design critiques, sprint planning, and product workshops
* Contribute to continuous improvements in product design processes
Design Systems & Innovation
* Help build and maintain scalable design systems and UI component libraries
* Promote consistent design patterns across multiple products
* Explore emerging design trends, AI-assisted workflows, and interaction models
* Advocate for human-centered design principles across the organization
* Mentor junior designers and contribute to a collaborative design culture
