About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management firm supporting innovative companies across blockchain, fintech, AI, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.
Through strategic investments and partnerships, we help accelerate the growth of decentralized technologies, digital asset ecosystems, and next-generation financial platforms. Our portfolio companies are building Web3 infrastructure, DeFi protocols, tokenized ecosystems, decentralized applications (dApps), and blockchain-powered products that are shaping the future of the internet.
We are seeking an experienced Senior Web3 Product Manager to lead product strategy, execution, and growth for innovative blockchain and Web3 products while working closely with engineering, design, business, and executive leadership.

The Opportunity
As a Senior Web3 Product Manager, you will own the product lifecycle from strategy through execution, helping transform complex blockchain technologies into intuitive, customer-focused products. You'll collaborate with Blockchain Engineers, Smart Contract Developers, Designers, Data Analysts, Marketing, and Executive Leadership to build scalable Web3 solutions that drive adoption and deliver measurable business value.
This role combines product leadership, strategic thinking, technical understanding, and market insight, offering the opportunity to shape products at the forefront of decentralized technology.
