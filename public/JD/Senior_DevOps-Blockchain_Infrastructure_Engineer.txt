Senior DevOps / Blockchain Infrastructure Engineer
Location: Australia / Remote
Employment Type: Full-time
Level: Senior / Staff
Industry: Web3 / FinTech / Blockchain Infrastructure
About Copia Group
Copia Group is building a Web3 fintech and payments infrastructure platform — essentially “Stripe for Web3.” Our goal is to make blockchain-based payments, financial infrastructure, and digital assets easier for businesses and users to integrate and operate at scale.
As we expand internationally, including our Australia operations, we're building a strong engineering team focused on reliable, secure, and scalable blockchain infrastructure.
The Role
We're looking for a Senior DevOps / Blockchain Infrastructure Engineer to take ownership of the infrastructure powering our Web3 platform.
You'll work across cloud infrastructure, blockchain nodes, deployment automation, observability, security, CI/CD, and reliability, partnering closely with backend, protocol, and smart-contract engineers.
This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys solving complex infrastructure problems and building systems that can operate reliably at scale.
What You'll Do
* Design, deploy, and maintain scalable infrastructure for blockchain and Web3 applications.
* Manage and operate blockchain nodes across EVM and other relevant networks.
* Build highly available and fault-tolerant infrastructure for blockchain RPC and API services.
* Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines for backend, smart-contract, and infrastructure deployments.
* Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration using Terraform, Ansible, or similar tools.
* Manage cloud infrastructure across AWS, GCP, or Azure.
* Containerize and orchestrate services using Docker and Kubernetes.
* Implement monitoring, logging, alerting, and observability for production systems.
* Improve system reliability, scalability, performance, and disaster recovery.
* Develop automation for blockchain node deployment, upgrades, backups, and health monitoring.
* Work with engineering teams to improve deployment and release processes.
* Help secure infrastructure, secrets, wallets, APIs, and blockchain-related services.
* Troubleshoot production incidents and participate in on-call / incident-response processes.
* Establish infrastructure standards and best practices as the engineering organization grows.
* Support infrastructure for Web3 payments, wallets, smart contracts, and other blockchain services.
