Location: Australia / Remote

About Copia Group
Copia Group is building a Web3 fintech and payments platform — essentially “Stripe for Web3.”
We're focused on making blockchain payments and financial infrastructure secure, reliable, scalable, and as easy to use as modern fintech products.
As we expand internationally, including our planned Australia presence, we're looking for a Senior QA Automation Engineer to help establish a high-quality engineering and testing culture across our Web3 platform.
About the Role
We're looking for a Senior QA Automation Engineer with strong experience in automation, API testing, distributed systems, and modern software quality practices.
You'll work closely with Backend, Frontend, Blockchain, DevOps, and Product teams to design and implement automated testing strategies for Web3 payments, APIs, smart-contract integrations, wallets, and financial workflows.
This is a hands-on role with significant ownership over our automation framework, testing infrastructure, CI/CD quality gates, and overall product reliability.
Key Responsibilities
* Design and maintain a comprehensive automated testing strategy across Web3 and fintech products.
* Build and maintain scalable UI, API, integration, and end-to-end automation frameworks.
* Test blockchain-related workflows including wallets, transactions, token transfers, payment flows, and blockchain integrations.
* Develop automated regression, smoke, integration, and system tests.
* Validate REST APIs, WebSocket services, microservices, and backend systems.
* Test interactions between traditional Web2 services and blockchain infrastructure.
* Integrate automated testing into CI/CD pipelines.
* Establish quality gates and automated release validation.
* Identify, reproduce, investigate, and document complex defects.
* Work closely with developers to perform root-cause analysis.
* Validate application behavior under different network and transaction conditions.
* Build test environments and test data strategies for automated testing.
* Monitor test stability and reduce flaky tests.
* Participate in architecture and product discussions from a quality perspective.
* Define QA standards, processes, and best practices as the engineering organization grows.
* Mentor other QA engineers and contribute to the development of the QA automation function.
