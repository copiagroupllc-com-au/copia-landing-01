About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management firm supporting innovative companies across artificial intelligence, fintech, blockchain, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.

The Opportunity
As a Senior AI Product Manager, you will own the end-to-end product lifecycle for AI and machine learning products—from discovery and roadmap planning to launch and continuous optimization. You'll work closely with AI Engineers, Data Scientists, Software Engineers, UX Designers, and business stakeholders to transform cutting-edge AI capabilities into scalable, customer-centric products.
This role combines strategic product thinking, technical understanding of AI systems, and strong execution skills, offering the opportunity to build impactful AI solutions across multiple industries.
