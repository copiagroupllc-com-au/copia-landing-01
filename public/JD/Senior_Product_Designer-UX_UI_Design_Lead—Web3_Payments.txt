Senior Product Designer / UX/UI Design Lead — Web3 Payments
Location: Australia / Remote
Employment Type: Full-time
Department: Product & Design
Level: Senior / Lead
About Copia Group
Copia Group is building a Web3 fintech and payments platform — essentially “Stripe for Web3.”
Our goal is to make blockchain-based payments and financial products feel as simple, intuitive, and trustworthy as the best modern fintech products. As we expand internationally, including our planned Australia presence, we're looking for a senior product designer who can help shape the user experience from the ground up.
About the Role
We're looking for a Senior Product Designer / UX/UI Design Lead to own the design experience across our Web3 payments platform.
You'll work closely with Product, Engineering, Business Development, and leadership to turn complex blockchain and financial workflows into simple, intuitive, and user-friendly experiences.
This is a hands-on leadership role. You'll have significant ownership over product discovery, UX strategy, design systems, prototyping, and the final product experience.
Key Responsibilities
* Own UX/UI design across key products and payment experiences.
* Translate complex Web3, blockchain, and fintech concepts into simple user experiences.
* Lead the design process from research → discovery → wireframes → prototypes → UI → production.
* Work closely with Product Managers and Engineers to define product requirements and user journeys.
* Conduct user research, interviews, usability testing, and competitive analysis.
* Create high-fidelity prototypes and production-ready designs in Figma.
* Build and evolve a scalable design system across web and mobile products.
* Establish UX standards and design principles for the organization.
* Collaborate with engineering teams to ensure designs are implemented accurately.
* Use product analytics and user feedback to continuously improve the experience.
* Mentor and provide design direction to other designers as the team grows.
* Contribute to product strategy and roadmap discussions.
* Help define the UX vision for Copia's Web3 payments ecosystem.
