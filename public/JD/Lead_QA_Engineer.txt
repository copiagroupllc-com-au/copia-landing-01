The Opportunity
As a Lead QA Engineer, you will be responsible for shaping quality engineering practices, leading testing initiatives, and ensuring the delivery of reliable, secure, and high-performing products.
You will collaborate closely with engineering, product, and DevOps teams to implement scalable quality processes, improve automation coverage, and champion a quality-first culture throughout the software development lifecycle.
This role combines hands-on testing expertise with leadership responsibilities and strategic influence across multiple teams.
