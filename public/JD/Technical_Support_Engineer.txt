About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management and technology-focused firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.
We collaborate on projects connected to Stripe’s ecosystem, helping build scalable payment systems, financial infrastructure, developer-focused platforms, and modern commerce solutions used by businesses globally.
We are growing our support and operations organization and seeking a Technical Support Engineer passionate about solving technical issues, improving customer experience, and supporting modern technology platforms at scale.

The Opportunity
We are seeking a Technical Support Engineer to help support and maintain fintech and commerce platforms used by businesses and developers worldwide.
In this role, you will work closely with engineering, product, and operations teams to troubleshoot technical issues, resolve platform incidents, and support customers and internal stakeholders across APIs, payment systems, and infrastructure services.
This is a highly collaborative role offering strong technical exposure, problem-solving opportunities, and long-term growth within a modern engineering environment.
