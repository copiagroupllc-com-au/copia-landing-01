About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management and technology-focused firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.
We collaborate on projects connected to Stripe’s ecosystem, helping build scalable payment systems, financial infrastructure, developer-focused platforms, and modern commerce solutions used by businesses globally.
We are expanding our security engineering organization and seeking experienced security professionals passionate about protecting modern platforms, cloud infrastructure, and financial technology systems.

The Opportunity
We are seeking a Senior Security Engineer to help secure modern fintech and commerce platforms across cloud infrastructure, applications, APIs, and internal systems.
In this role, you will work closely with engineering, DevOps, and product teams to strengthen platform security, improve security operations, and establish best practices across infrastructure, application development, and operational processes.
This is a high-impact role offering strong technical ownership, cross-functional collaboration, and the opportunity to shape security strategy across rapidly evolving technology platforms.
________________

Security Engineering & Operations
* Design, implement, and maintain security controls across applications, infrastructure, and cloud environments
* Identify, assess, and remediate security vulnerabilities across systems and services
* Conduct security reviews, risk assessments, and threat modeling exercises
* Monitor systems for security incidents, suspicious activity, and operational risks
* Support incident response, investigation, and remediation processes
Application & Infrastructure Security
* Improve security practices across APIs, authentication systems, and cloud infrastructure
* Collaborate with engineering teams to integrate security into development workflows and CI/CD pipelines
* Implement infrastructure hardening, access controls, and secrets management
* Support secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC) initiatives
* Conduct penetration testing coordination and security validation activities
Compliance & Best Practices
* Help establish and maintain security policies, standards, and operational procedures
* Support compliance initiatives related to fintech and infrastructure security requirements
* Improve security awareness and engineering best practices across teams
* Maintain security documentation, reporting, and remediation tracking
