Blockchain Engineering Lead
About Copia Group
Copia Group is building a next-generation Web3 fintech and payments platform — essentially “Stripe for Web3.”
Our goal is to make blockchain-based financial products easier to build, integrate, and use by combining blockchain infrastructure, payments, wallets, and intuitive product experiences.
As we expand globally, including our Australia operations, we're looking for an experienced Blockchain Engineering Lead to help shape and lead the engineering direction of our Web3 platform.
The Role
As Blockchain Engineering Lead, you will lead the development of blockchain-enabled products and services across our platform.
You will work across smart-contract integrations, Web3 applications, backend architecture, wallets, blockchain APIs, and engineering standards. This is a hands-on leadership role for someone who enjoys both building systems and guiding engineering decisions.
You will work closely with product, frontend, backend, and other technical stakeholders to turn complex blockchain capabilities into reliable, production-ready financial products.
Key Responsibilities
* Lead the technical development of blockchain and Web3 components across the Copia platform.
* Design and implement scalable Web3 application architecture.
* Lead integrations with EVM-compatible blockchains, wallets, smart contracts, and third-party Web3 infrastructure.
* Design and maintain blockchain-enabled backend services and REST APIs.
* Guide smart-contract integration patterns and Web3 transaction flows.
* Build reliable wallet connection, signing, transaction, and asset-management workflows.
* Establish engineering standards for security, testing, code quality, and maintainability.
* Review architecture, technical designs, and pull requests.
* Mentor and support other blockchain and full-stack engineers.
* Work with product and design teams to translate Web3 requirements into practical technical solutions.
* Investigate blockchain transaction issues, integration failures, and production incidents.
* Help define the technical roadmap for Copia's blockchain engineering capabilities.
* Evaluate new blockchain protocols, Web3 infrastructure providers, developer tools, and emerging technologies.
* Contribute hands-on code to critical blockchain and backend components.
