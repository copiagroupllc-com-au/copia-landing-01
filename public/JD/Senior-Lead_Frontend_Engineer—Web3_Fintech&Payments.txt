Senior / Lead Frontend Engineer — Web3 Fintech & Payments
Company: Copia Group, LLC
Location: Australia / Remote
Industry: Web3 · Fintech · Digital Asset Payments
About Copia Group
Copia Group is building a Web3 fintech and payments platform — essentially “Stripe for Web3.”
Our goal is to make digital-asset payments, wallets, financial products, and blockchain-based services easier for businesses and users to integrate and use.
As we expand globally, including our presence in Australia, we're building a strong engineering team focused on scalable, secure, and intuitive Web3 financial products.
About the Role
We are looking for a Senior / Lead Frontend Engineer to help shape and build the user-facing experience of our Web3 fintech platform.
This is more than a UI implementation role. You will have an opportunity to influence product architecture, technical decisions, UX, engineering standards, and the overall product direction.
The ideal candidate is someone who can work comfortably across frontend, mobile, backend integrations, and product development — and who enjoys working in a startup environment where engineers have significant ownership.

* Build high-quality web applications using React, TypeScript and Next.js.

* Integrate frontend applications with APIs, wallets, blockchain infrastructure, and financial services.
* Work with product and design teams to translate requirements into intuitive user experiences.
* Participate in technical architecture and product decisions.
* Help establish engineering standards, testing practices, documentation, and development workflows.

* Contribute to Web3 features including wallets, transactions, payments, digital assets, and blockchain integrations.
* Troubleshoot performance and scalability issues across web and mobile applications.
* Mentor other engineers and contribute to the technical growth of the team.
* Take ownership of features from initial concept through production.
