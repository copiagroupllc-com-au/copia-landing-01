About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, blockchain, AI, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.
Through strategic investments and partnerships, we help accelerate the growth of technology-driven businesses by providing strategic guidance and operational support. Our portfolio companies build secure, scalable software platforms that demand exceptional quality, reliability, and performance.
We are seeking an experienced Head of Quality Assurance (QA) to define and execute our quality strategy, build world-class QA organizations, and establish engineering practices that ensure every product meets the highest standards before reaching customers.

The Opportunity
As the Head of QA, you will lead the organization's quality engineering function, overseeing test strategy, automation, release quality, and continuous improvement across multiple product teams. You'll partner closely with Engineering, Product, DevOps, Security, and Executive Leadership to create a quality-first culture and scalable testing practices.
This is a leadership role for someone who combines deep technical expertise with operational excellence and a passion for mentoring high-performing QA teams.
