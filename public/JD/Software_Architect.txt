About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management and technology-focused firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.

The Opportunity
We are seeking a Software Architect to lead architecture strategy and system design across complex fintech and commerce platforms.
In this role, you will define scalable technical solutions, guide engineering teams through architectural decisions, and help establish long-term engineering standards across backend systems, APIs, cloud infrastructure, and distributed applications.
You will work closely with engineering leadership, product stakeholders, and cross-functional teams to ensure systems are scalable, secure, maintainable, and aligned with business goals.
This is a high-impact leadership role with broad technical ownership and strategic influence across the organization.
