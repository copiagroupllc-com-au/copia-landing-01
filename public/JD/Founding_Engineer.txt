About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management and technology-focused firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, digital infrastructure, and emerging technology sectors.
We collaborate on projects connected to Stripe’s ecosystem, helping build scalable payment systems, developer-focused platforms, financial infrastructure, and next-generation commerce solutions used by businesses globally.
We are building a new generation of fintech and infrastructure products and are seeking a Founding Engineer to help shape both the technical foundation and long-term product vision of the platform.

The Opportunity
This is a rare opportunity to join at the earliest stage of product and engineering development as a Founding Engineer.
