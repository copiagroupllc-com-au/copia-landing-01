The Opportunity
As a Senior Developer Relations Engineer, you will serve as both a technical expert and community advocate. You'll engage directly with developers, create technical content, build demos and sample applications, and provide valuable feedback to product and engineering teams.
You'll help developers successfully adopt platforms and APIs while influencing product direction through community insights. This role combines software engineering, technical communication, community engagement, and developer advocacy.
If you're passionate about technology, education, and helping developers succeed, this role offers a unique opportunity to make a meaningful impact.
