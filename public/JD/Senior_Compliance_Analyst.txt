About The Copia Group
The Copia Group is an investment management firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, digital infrastructure, blockchain, AI, and emerging technology sectors.
Through strategic investments and partnerships, we help accelerate the growth of high-potential businesses while maintaining a strong commitment to regulatory compliance, operational integrity, and effective risk management. As our portfolio continues to expand, we are seeking experienced professionals who can help strengthen our compliance framework and support responsible business operations.
We are looking for a Senior Compliance Analyst to help ensure our business activities and portfolio operations remain aligned with applicable regulations, internal policies, and industry best practices.
________________


The Opportunity
As a Senior Compliance Analyst, you will play a critical role in supporting compliance programs, regulatory reporting, internal controls, and risk management initiatives. You will work closely with Legal, Finance, Operations, Investment, and Technology teams to identify compliance risks, improve governance processes, and ensure adherence to evolving regulatory requirements.
This role is ideal for an analytical professional who enjoys solving complex regulatory challenges while contributing to a culture of integrity, transparency, and operational excellence.
________________


What You'll Do
Regulatory Compliance
* Monitor and interpret applicable regulatory requirements affecting business operations
* Support the implementation and maintenance of compliance policies and procedures
* Conduct compliance reviews, control testing, and regulatory assessments
* Assist with regulatory filings, reporting obligations, and audit requests
* Monitor regulatory developments and recommend necessary policy updates
Risk Management & Governance
* Identify compliance risks and recommend practical mitigation strategies
* Perform risk assessments across operational, financial, and technology processes
* Evaluate internal controls and recommend process improvements
* Assist in developing governance frameworks and compliance monitoring programs
* Support enterprise risk management initiatives
Monitoring & Investigations
* Conduct compliance monitoring and periodic internal reviews
* Investigate compliance issues, policy violations, and operational concerns
* Document findings and prepare recommendations for corrective actions
* Track remediation efforts and ensure timely resolution of identified issues
* Support internal and external audits
Cross-Functional Collaboration
* Partner with Legal, Finance, Operations, and Technology teams on compliance initiatives
* Provide compliance guidance during new product launches and business initiatives
* Deliver compliance training and awareness programs for employees
* Prepare reports and presentations for senior leadership
* Promote a culture of ethical business conduct and regulatory awareness
