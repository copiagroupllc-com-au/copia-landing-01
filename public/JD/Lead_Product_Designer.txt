The Copia Group is an investment management firm supporting innovative companies across fintech, digital infrastructure, commerce, and emerging technology sectors.
Through strategic investments and partnerships, we help advance the development of modern financial services, developer platforms, AI-driven solutions, and digital products that power businesses around the world.

The Opportunity
As a Lead Product Designer, you will play a strategic role in shaping the user experience of innovative web and mobile products. You'll partner closely with Product Managers, Engineers, Researchers, and business stakeholders to transform complex challenges into elegant, scalable design solutions.
Beyond designing exceptional interfaces, you'll help define design processes, mentor designers, evolve our design system, and influence product direction from discovery through delivery.
This is a leadership opportunity for a designer who enjoys balancing creativity, business thinking, and technical collaboration.
