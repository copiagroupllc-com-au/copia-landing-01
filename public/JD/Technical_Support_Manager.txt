The Opportunity
As a Technical Support Manager, you will lead a team of Technical Support Engineers responsible for delivering outstanding customer experiences and resolving complex technical issues. You will oversee support operations, improve processes, establish service standards, and collaborate with Engineering, Product, and Customer Success teams to enhance product quality and customer satisfaction.
This role combines technical expertise, operational leadership, and people management, making it ideal for someone passionate about building efficient support organizations and helping customers succeed.
